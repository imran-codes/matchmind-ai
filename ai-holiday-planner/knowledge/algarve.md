# Algarve

The Algarve is a classic family-friendly destination in southern Portugal.

## Family suitability

Many resorts offer calm beaches, apartment-style accommodation, and easy access
to family activities.

## Transfers

Transfer times vary depending on whether you stay near Faro or further west.

Resorts closer to Faro generally have the shortest transfer times.

## Climate

Late spring and summer are usually the best periods for beach holidays.

## Considerations

Popular resort towns can become busy during school holiday periods.

Choosing the right resort matters as some areas are quieter than others.
