# Majorca

Majorca is a Mediterranean destination popular with UK families.

## Family suitability

Alcudia is particularly suitable for young families because of its shallow beaches
and availability of family-oriented accommodation.

## Transfers

Transfer times from Palma Airport vary considerably by resort.

Palma Nova can typically be reached considerably faster than Alcudia.

## Climate

June is normally warm and suitable for beach holidays.

## Considerations

School holiday periods are significantly busier.

Popular family resorts should be booked early.
