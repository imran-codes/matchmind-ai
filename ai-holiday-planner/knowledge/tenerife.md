# Tenerife

Tenerife is a year-round Atlantic destination with strong family appeal.

## Family suitability

Costa Adeje is often preferred by families because of its beaches,
resorts, and broad range of hotels.

## Transfers

South airport transfers are typically shorter than those from the north.

Resorts in the south are usually the most convenient for family holidays.

## Climate

Tenerife generally offers warm weather outside the peak summer period.

## Considerations

The island can be windy in some areas.

Checking resort location carefully helps avoid longer transfer times.
